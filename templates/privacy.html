Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 31, 2026

Data We Collect

When you use Spendly, we collect the information you provide directly, such as your name, email address, and the expense records you log — including amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions. We may also collect basic technical information, such as browser type and device information, to keep the service running reliably.

How We Use Your Data

We use your data to provide core features of the service: displaying your expenses, generating spending summaries, and maintaining your account. We do not use your financial data for advertising, and we do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Data Storage

Your account and expense data are stored securely and retained for as long as your account remains active. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time, after which your records will be permanently removed from our systems.

Third Party Services

Spendly may rely on third-party providers for infrastructure such as hosting and email delivery. These providers only process data as necessary to support the service and are not permitted to use your information for their own purposes.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us at support@spendly.app.
